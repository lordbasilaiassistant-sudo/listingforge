    Questions

    Is ListingForge really free? +

    Yes. The free tier runs 100% in your browser and builds a complete SEO listing skeleton — title, bullets, and ranked keyword tags — from your notes, with no key and no signup. Optional Pro mode lets you paste your own GLM key for AI-written copy.

    Does it follow each marketplace's rules? +

    Yes. Etsy: 140-char titles and 13 tags (max 20 chars each). Amazon: 200-char titles, 5 bullets, plus backend search terms. eBay: 80-char titles and item-specific keyword phrases. The generator trims and ranks to fit each.

    Where does my data and API key go? +

    Nowhere we can see. There's no backend. The free generator never leaves your browser. In Pro mode your key is stored only in your browser's localStorage and sent only to the official z.ai GLM endpoint over HTTPS.

    How are keywords ranked? +

    ListingForge extracts single and multi-word phrases, scores them by frequency, position, specificity, and buyer-intent signals (gift, handmade, custom, etc.), removes stop-words and duplicates, then trims to each marketplace's tag rules so your strongest terms rank first.

    Will it write spammy, generic copy? +

    No. The free build is structured from your real inputs — no filler. The AI rewrite uses a strict prompt and a quality gate that rejects bland, repetitive output and regenerates sharper, specific copy.
